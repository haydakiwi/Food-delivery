## 1. Team Values

## 2. Meetings

- The goal is to have at least one meeting per week. Whenever we run into an error or have anything important to discuss, we will hold another meeting as soon as possible.
- We will meet either online on Discord or in person in a study room, library, or Fipke.
- Arrive on time and be prepared for meetings.
- If unable to attend:
  - Notify the team as early as possible.
  - Provide updates on assigned work.
  - Review any important information from the missed meeting.
- If someone cannot attend, we will send them a summary of the meeting, queries brought up, and any tasks they may have been assigned.

## 3. Communication

- We use iMessage and a Discord server to communicate with each other.
- iMessage is the quickest way for us to communicate because it is an app we all have on our phones.
- Use the group chat to:
  - Share updates.
  - Ask questions.
  - Share files and resources.
  - Discuss deadlines and responsibilities.
- Use Discord, iMessage, or Canvas messages for urgent matters and clearly indicate when something is urgent.
- Important team information will be shared in the general chat on our Discord lab server. A message will also be sent through the iMessage group chat to notify the team.

## 4. Response Expectations

- Respond to team messages within 24 hours whenever possible.
- If unable to respond within 24 hours:
  - Notify the team as soon as possible.
  - Explain when you expect to be available.
- If a member repeatedly does not respond:
  - Contact them directly to understand the situation.
  - Discuss the issue with the team if it continues.
  - Involve the instructor or TA if the lack of communication affects the project.

## 5. Respect & Professional Conduct

- Respect different opinions, ideas, and approaches.
- Give everyone an opportunity to contribute.
- Focus criticism on ideas or work rather than individuals.
- Avoid:
  - Insults or disrespectful behaviour.
  - Intentionally excluding team members.
  - Taking credit for someone else's work.
  - Ignoring reasonable concerns.
  - Preventing someone from participating.
- Maintain a professional and supportive team environment.

## 6. Accountability

If a team member does not meet an agreed expectation, the team will first contact them privately to understand the situation. The member will explain the issue and work with the team to create a reasonable recovery plan with a revised deadline.

If the problem continues, responsibilities may be reassigned to protect the team's progress. Repeated issues will be documented and discussed with the instructor or TA if they negatively affect the project.

## 7. Decision-Making

The team will aim to make decisions through consensus after giving every member an opportunity to share their opinion.

If consensus cannot be reached within a reasonable amount of time, the team will decide through a majority vote, with each member receiving one vote.

In the event of a tie, the team will review the available evidence, project requirements, and deadlines before voting again. If a serious disagreement remains unresolved, the team will seek guidance from the instructor or TA.

## 8. Conflict Resolution

- Talk about the matter politely and directly.
- Allow everyone to share their viewpoints.
- Focus on the problem rather than personal criticism.
- Determine potential solutions together.
- Decide on a solution and record any next steps.
- Ask the TA or instructor for help if disagreements cannot be solved internally.

## 9. Anticipated Challenges and Solutions

- **Missed Meetings:** The member must review the meeting notes and contact the team to stay informed about decisions and assigned tasks.
- **Missed Deadline:** The member should immediately notify the group and work together to develop a recovery plan.
- **Unequal Contributions:** The team will discuss expectations, monitor progress, and reassign tasks as necessary to ensure a fair workload.
- **Insufficient Communication:** Team members are expected to check Discord and iMessage regularly and respond within the agreed timeframe. Reminders will be sent as needed.
- **Schedule Conflicts:** Meetings will be planned in advance, and times that work for everyone will be selected using a planner.
- **Disagreements:** Members will work towards a respectful and constructive solution.
- **General Team Challenges:** The team will deal with problems early, speak honestly, and work together to identify workable solutions.
